# Undergraduate Research Posters

Poster presentations from my undergraduate research at Manhattan University (2024).

## Exploring Sentiment Analysis on Social Media Data: The Example of Tweets during the 2020 US Election on X

- **Advisor:** Prof. Alexander Adesman
- Poster and oral presentation, Manhattan University undergraduate research, 2024
- Analyzed sentiment in over 600,000 tweets from the 2020 U.S. presidential election (Python, Tweepy, TextBlob, VADER), designed bot-detection heuristics after finding automated posts distorted aggregate statistics, and tracked sentiment shifts against political events.
- [Poster PDF](sentiment-analysis-poster-2024.pdf)

## Cloud Computing Adoption and Improvement in Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

- **Advisor:** Prof. Wafa Elmannai
- Poster and oral presentation, Manhattan University undergraduate research, 2024
- Studied barriers to cloud-computing adoption by small and mid-sized enterprises (data security, unpredictable costs) and built a C++ simulation of how improved security and reduced redundant adoption affect annual profit.
- [Poster PDF](cloud-computing-poster-2024.pdf)
